What the rules saw

    A replay, not a prediction

    The rules act on completed daily bars. A signal made at the close may fill no earlier than the next trading day's open. Daily OHLC does not reveal intraday price order; ambiguous stops take priority. Provider adjustments and broker spreads are assumptions, not historical executions.
